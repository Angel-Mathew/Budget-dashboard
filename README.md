
# Budget Dashboard

A dashboard that shows how a budget is split between departments and where the money is going. I cleaned and prepared the data with SQL, then built the visuals in Power BI with KPI cards, bar charts and a map.

## The problem
Budget figures are hard to compare when they sit in a spreadsheet. I wanted managers to see at a glance which departments get the biggest share and to click into any one of them.

## What you can explore
- Budget by department (clickable department cards)
- A bar chart that updates when you pick a department
- A location map showing where each budget sits

## What I found
Across the five departments, the total budget is 4.25M.
- Engineering is the largest at 1.5M (about 35 percent of the total).
- Finance follows at 950K, then Marketing at 800K and Sales at 600K.
- Human Resources is the smallest at 400K.

## Tools
SQL and Power BI
